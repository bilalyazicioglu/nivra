# Community code of conduct

Treat participants with respect regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint. Give actionable feedback, assume good intent where reasonable, and respect personal boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, publishing private information, and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and protect affected participants. Maintainers are held to the same standards.

For a sensitive report, request a private contact channel without posting the underlying details publicly. GitHub's report-content tools are also available. Do not expose someone else's private information in a report.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces. It does not claim governance over unrelated personal activity.
